FireOps Trainer — quick guide

Set up: Search or 📍 locate to fly to the training property. Set wind by dragging the compass needle (needle points where wind is FROM) and the speed slider.

Start fire: Pick 🔥 Ignite and tap/drag on the map, then press ▶. Fire spreads with the wind; change wind mid-scenario to test crews' reactions.

Resources: Tap a unit in the bottom bar, then tap the map to place it. Drag units to move them. Tap a unit for options — toggle Working and units knock down fire within their circle. Drag the 🚜 dozer to cut line as it moves. ✈️ tanker has a Drop retardant button.

Tools: 🚧 paints fire line (fuel break), 🌿 paints fuel types, 🧯 erases fire, ⭕ cycles brush size. 🪢 Hose: drag along the route — the lay shows length in feet; tap it to set diameter & GPM for friction loss.

Structure & EMS: Unit tabs switch between Wildland, Structure and EMS kits. A 🏠🔥 Str. fire progresses smoke showing → working → fully involved on the scenario clock unless Working units close by knock it down; fully involved buildings throw embers downwind, and a wildland fire reaching an 🏠 Exposure sets it alight. 🚰 hydrants, 🛢️ drop tanks, 🚩 staging, LZ and 🤕 patients all take notes (tap any unit) for scripting calls.

Spread rates are calibrated to published grass-fire behavior (head fire ≈ 15–20% of the 10-m wind; Anderson ellipse shapes; slow sheltered timber). No slope, spotting or fuel-moisture effects — a training discussion driver, not a prediction tool.
